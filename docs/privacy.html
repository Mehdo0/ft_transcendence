SEO Inspector — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026

What data do we collect?

Nothing. Zero. Nada.

SEO Inspector does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information whatsoever.

How does it work?

All SEO analysis is performed entirely locally in your browser. When you click the extension icon, it reads the HTML of the current page and analyzes it using your browser's built-in DOM parser. No data is ever sent to any server, API, or third party.

What about analytics?

We don't use any analytics, tracking, cookies, or fingerprinting. We have no idea who you are, what pages you analyze, or how often you use the extension — and we don't want to know.

What about the optional backend API?

The extension can optionally connect to a backend API for richer analysis. This feature is disabled by default and requires explicit user configuration. Unless you voluntarily set it up, it never activates.

Third-party services

None. We don't use Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Sentry, or any other third-party service. The extension is fully self-contained.

Your data rights

Since we collect nothing, there's nothing to access, delete, or port. You have nothing to worry about.
